PROJECT GOAL
===========================================================

Develop an end-to-end platform capable of:

• Monitoring financial transactions
• Detecting suspicious mule account behavior
• Building relationship graphs
• Displaying geo intelligence
• Performing AI risk scoring
• Supporting fraud investigation
• Providing an AI Copilot for investigators

The project should be hackathon-ready while maintaining production architecture.
